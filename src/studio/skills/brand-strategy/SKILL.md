# Brand Strategy
## Use When
Defining a brand or campaign strategy before copy or visual execution.
## Inputs
Brief, known evidence, audience context, constraints, and desired outcome.
## Process
1. Define the objective and audience.
2. State the audience tension or problem.
3. Define brand promise, positioning, differentiation, and reasons to believe.
4. Set the key message and communication territory.
5. Translate the strategy into creative implications.
## Output
Objective, audience, tension, promise, positioning, differentiation, evidence limits, key message, territory, and creative implications.
## Validation
Check SRDDA: specific, relevant, distinctive, defensible, actionable.
## Avoid
Generic branding language, unsupported claims, or invented evidence.
